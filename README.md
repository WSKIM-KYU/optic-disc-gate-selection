# optic-disc-gate-selection

Code, pre-registrations, locked results and figures for:
H. R. Cho and W. S. Kim, "Selecting Optic-Disc Localisers for Unseen Fundus Cameras Without Labels: Underspecification, the Limits of Test-Time Consistency and a Deployment-Gate Criterion" (submitted to IEEE JBHI).

- `code/`: evaluation, analysis and figure scripts (`p2c_*` Chaksu, `p2g_*` RIGA, `p2p_*` PALM, `p2e_*` exploratory) and the frame-estimator code.
- `results/`: reference lock files, pre-registrations, self-test logs, locked results (JSON) and SHA-256 records. Locked files are byte-identical to those used in the paper.
- `figures/`: Figures 1-5.

No fundus images, masks, image crops or model weights are included, because the dataset terms (Chaksu, RIGA/Messidor, PALM, FIVES, IDRiD, PAPILA, RIM-ONE DL) do not permit redistribution of images or derived image sets. Obtain the datasets from their distributors.
